Use this folder to track **publicly available** concepts from other 2026 teams when they materially affect differentiation.

## Research rules

- Use only public team/project information.
- Record the source URL and access date.
- Focus on idea overlap, technical approach and differentiation—not personal judgments about teams.
- Do not treat a public concept as proof of implementation quality.
- Re-check close competitors after the full challenge briefs/resources are released.
