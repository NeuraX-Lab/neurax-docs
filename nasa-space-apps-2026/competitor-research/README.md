# Competitor Research — NASA Space Apps 2026

Use this folder to track **publicly available** concepts from other 2026 teams when they materially affect differentiation.

## Research rules

- Use only public team/project information.
- Record the source URL and access date.
- Focus on idea overlap, technical approach and differentiation—not personal judgments about teams.
- Do not treat a public concept as proof of implementation quality.
- Re-check close competitors after the full challenge briefs/resources are released.

## Current implications for NeuraX

The MODIS/VIIRS challenge already has public concepts combining some of the following:

- MODIS/VIIRS reconciliation;
- historical fire calendars;
- weather/context variables;
- prediction or risk mapping.

Therefore `harmonization + prediction + dashboard` alone should not be considered a strong differentiator for NeuraX FireSense.

Detailed source-by-source notes can be added here during the 2026-10-28 challenge audit.
