## 5. Why AI / geospatial / simulation?

Do not add AI, mapping, hardware or simulation only for novelty. Explain the specific reasoning, prediction, retrieval, classification, segmentation, ranking, optimization, scenario-analysis or spatial-analysis job that cannot be replaced by a simpler approach without losing meaningful user value.

## 7. Demo test

A strong hackathon direction should answer yes to most of these:

- Can we explain the problem in under 30 seconds?
- Can the core workflow be demonstrated in 2–3 minutes?
- Can we build a credible end-to-end prototype within the event window?
- Is NASA/open data central rather than decorative?
- Can judges understand the impact without reading a paper first?
- Can we show a concrete result instead of only architecture slides?
- Can we measure or validate the result?
- Can the user make a better decision or take an action because of the product?
